Cherry Turnovers recipe

Skip the bakery and make these easy cherry turnovers at home with store-bought shortcuts: refrigerated pie dough and prepared cherry pie filling

Golden, flaky, and filled with sweet cherries, they’re a deliciously lazy way to enjoy a bakery-style treat in minutes.

Preparation Time 10 mins
Cook Time 15 mins
Total Time 25 mins
Servings 8

Ingredients

  • 1 (14.1 ounce) box refrigerated pie crust (such as Pillsbury)
  • 1 (14 ounce) can cherry pie filling
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons cinnamon sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon water

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Dust a clean work surface lightly with flour and lay out the pie dough. Use a rolling pin to roll pie dough until 1/8-inch thick. Cut circles out of the dough using a soup bowl. Pull away extra dough and set circles aside. Press extra dough together and roll out again. Repeat the process until all the dough is used up.
  3. Pour cherry pie filling into a bowl and stir in vanilla extract. Mash pie filling with a fork to break up cherries. .
  4. Whisk egg and water together in a small bowl.
  5. Lay one 1 circle of dough on a lightly floured surface. Spoon a generous portion of cherry filling onto the bottom half of the dough circle, leaving a 1-inch border free. Fold top half of dough over the filling and press edges together. Crimp edges with a fork all the way around.
  6. Lightly brush cherry turnover with egg wash and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar. Set onto the prepared baking sheet. Repeat with remaining ingredients.
  7. Bake turnovers in the preheated oven until golden brown, 15 to 20 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.
